What the show is

India Treasury Brief is a twice-weekly, ten-minute podcast for Indian corporate treasurers: group and regional treasurers, CFOs and controllers.

One theme per episode: what's moving for corporate treasury in India, and what to do about it. Every episode is built around one question a treasurer would ask, and closes with something concrete to act on, not just why the story matters.

Built from exchange filings, regulator circulars, RBI data and public reporting. Sources are listed in every episode's notes.
