What the show is

India Treasury Brief is a twice-weekly, ten-minute podcast for India's treasury and finance teams, from big corporates to startups: group and regional treasurers, CFOs and controllers, and the FP&A and treasury people at startups and smaller companies.

One theme per episode: what's moving for treasury in India, and what to do about it. Every episode is built around one question a treasurer would ask, and closes with something concrete to act on, not just why the story matters.

Episodes cover what changes for a smaller company or a startup, and some are built for them: episodes for startups and SMEs.

Built from exchange filings, regulator circulars, RBI data and public reporting. Sources are listed in every episode's notes.
