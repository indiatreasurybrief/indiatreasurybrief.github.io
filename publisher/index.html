ITB Publisher

The show's private publishing tool

ITB Publisher is a small command-line tool that India Treasury Brief uses to publish its own episodes and posts. Only the show's operator uses it, signed in to the show's own accounts. It isn't available to anyone else, and it never accesses other people's accounts.

What it does

  • YouTube: uploads the show's episodes and Shorts to the show's channel, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles and playlist, and reads the channel's YouTube Analytics for the show's own reviews.
  • Instagram and X: posts the show's own Reels, carousels and posts to its own accounts, and reads their statistics.

How it's used

  • Every post is written and read by a person before it goes out. The tool prints exactly what it will send, and posts only when told to.
  • It publishes only content the show made itself.
  • Sign-in tokens stay on the operator's computer.

How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy. Contact: indiatreasurybrief@gmail.com.
